# DealShield AI — Security, Privacy & Integrity Architecture

## 1. Security Architecture & Boundary Management

DealShield AI processes commercially sensitive negotiations. Protecting client privacy, preserving evidence authenticity, and securing API credentials are top design priorities.

### Credential Separation Matrix

| Credential | Scope | Location | Access Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` | Backend Only | `apps/api/.env` | **CRITICAL: NEVER expose to frontend or git.** Bypasses RLS to write audit logs and access storage. |
| `GEMINI_API_KEY` / `OPENAI_API_KEY` | Backend Only | `apps/api/.env` | Never exposed to browser. Used only by backend LLM extractor (Gemini 3.8 Flash). |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` | Frontend & Public | `apps/web/.env.local` | Subject to strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` | Frontend & Public | `apps/web/.env.local` | Public API endpoint address. |

## 2. Row Level Security (RLS) & Authorization

Every table in Supabase PostgreSQL enforces RLS:

### Data Tables & Access Boundaries
1. **`users`:**
   - Authenticated user can `SELECT` and `UPDATE` only their own profile: `auth.uid() = id`.
2. **`conversations`:**
   - Owned by the uploading user: `auth.uid() = user_id`.
   - `DELETE` cascade removes child messages and analyses.
3. **`messages` & `analyses`:**
   - Access verified through conversation ownership:
     `EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM conversations WHERE conversations.id = messages.conversation_id AND conversations.user_id = auth.uid())`.
4. **`agreements` & `agreement_versions`:**
   - Accessible via the conversation ownership chain.
5. **`confirmations`:**
   - Readable by either confirmed party with an authenticated session.
6. **`audit_events` (Append-Only):**
   - `SELECT` allowed for authenticated entity owners.
   - `INSERT` is strictly blocked for anon/authenticated roles—allowed **only via backend service role**.

### Private Storage Buckets
- Bucket `raw-chats`: Private. Accessible only via signed URLs generated by the backend.
- Bucket `agreements-pdf`: Private. Accessible only by verified parties via time-limited signed download URLs (expiry: 15 minutes).

## 3. Cryptographic Hashes vs Legal Signatures & Stamp Duty

> [!WARNING]
> **Statutory Limitation Notice:**
> A cryptographic hash only establishes **data consistency against a trusted reference**. Do not confuse mathematical hashing with statutory legal execution or stamped instruments.

### What SHA-256 Proves:
- The exact bitstream of an agreement or chat transcript has not been altered since the hash was recorded.
- Provides corroborative electronic record evidence under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 / Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

### What SHA-256 Does NOT Prove:
- **Identity:** A hash does not prove who authored or uploaded the text.
- **Contractual Intention:** A hash does not prove mutual consensus ad idem.
- **Statutory eSign:** A hash does not satisfy the criteria of an electronic signature under Section 3 / Section 3A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, which requires asymmetric crypto issued by a licensed Certifying Authority (CCA).

### Indian Stamp Act, 1899 & Legal Enforceability:
- DealShield AI synthesizes a **plain-language summary agreement and term sheet**.
- It is **not** an executed legal deed and does not pay state or central stamp duty under the **Indian Stamp Act, 1899**. In Indian courts, unstamped or defectively stamped instruments cannot be admitted into evidence until requisite duty and penalties are impounded and satisfied.

## 4. Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Conversations uploaded to DealShield AI contain private commercial conversations and personal data governed by the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023)**.

### DPDP Act 2023 Obligations:
- **Purpose Limitation & Notice:** Users uploading transcripts must warrant consent from all participants for the specific purpose of agreement summarization (Section 6, DPDP Act 2023).
- **Salted IP Anonymization:** Raw IP addresses are never recorded in audit logs. The backend applies secret-salted HMAC-SHA256 (`hash_ip()` using `IP_HASH_SALT`) prior to persistence.
- **Retention Limitation:** Raw chat files are retained in private storage for 30 days by default, after which they are purged unless marked for legal hold (Section 8(7), DPDP Act 2023).
- **Right to Erasure:** Deleting a conversation triggers an immediate cascading purge of raw text, messages, and unconfirmed drafts (Section 12, DPDP Act 2023).
- **Fictional Development Data:** Real personal chats must never be committed to git. All tests and fixtures use synthetic fictional dialogues.

## 5. Counterparty Access & Email-OTP Confirmation Model

Negotiating counterparties typically lack an account on the platform. To guarantee verifiable mutual assent under Section 7 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 without compromising draft confidentiality or security, DealShield AI implements an **Email-OTP Counterparty Review & Confirmation Architecture**:

### 5.1 Architecture & Security Rules

1. **Mandatory Authenticated Link Creation:**
   - The contract initiator must be authenticated via Bearer token to generate a review link (`POST /api/v1/confirmations/tokens`).
   - The initiator explicitly declares the counterparty's email address (`party_email`), verified to differ from their own email.
   - Link generation returns a raw secret token (`/review?token=...`) without exposing the internal agreement UUID in the URL.

2. **Email Storage, Privacy & Zero PII Exposure:**
   - Plaintext emails are **never** stored directly in the database, logged in audit events, or exposed in error responses.
   - `party_email_encrypted`: Email encrypted symmetrically using Fernet (`ENCRYPTION_KEY`).
   - `party_email_hash`: Blind-index HMAC-SHA256 keyed with `SECRET_KEY`, enabling exact match queries without decryption.
   - In all public confirmation responses and audit metadata, emails are masked (e.g. `c***y@e***e.com`).

3. **Side-Effect-Free Link Inspection:**
   - `GET /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}` is strictly read-only and safe against browser and chat app prefetching (e.g. WhatsApp link previews).
   - Draft text and agreement clauses are **completely withheld** from this response until a valid review session is established.

4. **Step 1: Email-Check Verification & Lockout:**
   - The counterparty submits their email via `POST /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}/email-check`.
   - The input is normalized (`strip().lower()`) and compared against `party_email_hash` using constant-time `hmac.compare_digest`.
   - Mismatch returns a generic error (*"We couldn't verify this email"*) without disclosing the registered address.
   - Max 5 failed email check attempts per token. Exceeding this limit locks the token (`locked_at`) and returns HTTP 423.

5. **Step 2: Single-Use Cryptographic OTP Issuance:**
   - The counterparty requests an OTP via `POST /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}/otp/send`.
   - The OTP is **always** delivered to the decrypted stored email—never to an arbitrary client-supplied address.
   - Rate limits: Maximum 3 sends per rolling 15-minute window; minimum 30 seconds interval between resends (HTTP 429).
   - OTP is a 6-digit cryptographic number (`secrets.randbelow(1_000_000)`), stored as a SHA-256 digest (`otp_hash`) with a 10-minute TTL (`otp_expires_at`).

6. **Step 3: OTP Verification & Ephemeral Session Tokens:**
   - The counterparty submits their code via `POST /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}/otp/verify`.
   - Max 5 wrong attempts per issued OTP immediately invalidates the OTP.
   - Max 10 cumulative wrong attempts locks the confirmation row permanently (`locked_at`, HTTP 423).
   - Upon successful verification, a cryptographically secure 32-byte session token is generated (`secrets.token_urlsafe(32)`), stored as SHA-256 (`session_token_hash`), with a 30-minute TTL (`SESSION_TTL_MINUTES`).

7. **Step 4: Session-Gated Review & Actions (`X-Review-Session`):**
   - The counterparty passes the session token via the `X-Review-Session` HTTP header.
   - All subsequent operations require an active, unexpired session:
     - Accessing draft clauses (`GET /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}`)
     - Recording assent (`POST /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}`)
     - Declining terms with reason (`POST /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}`)
     - Downloading agreement PDF (`GET /api/v1/confirmations/token/{token}/download`)
   - Frontends store the session token purely in React memory state (`useState`), never in `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`.

8. **Direct Initiator Assent Restriction:**
   - Direct dashboard assent (`POST /api/v1/confirmations`) requires a valid Bearer token and is restricted exclusively to `agreement.parties[0]` (the initiator), unless `ALLOW_DIRECT_CONFIRM = True` is explicitly enabled for testing. All counterparties must verify through the Email-OTP link.

9. **Append-Only Tamper-Evident Auditing:**
   - Successful assent stamps the exact agreement version number, agreement SHA-256 content hash, client IP hash, and verification method into `confirmations` and append-only `audit_events`.
   - Both parties receive confirmation email receipts with audit identifiers upon completion.

## 6. Input Validation & Defense in Depth

1. **Path Traversal Prevention:** Uploaded filenames are sanitized (`sanitize_filename()`) to strip path separators and non-standard symbols.
2. **10 MB File Limit Enforced:** Upload endpoints strictly reject files or payloads exceeding 10 MB with HTTP 413.
3. **Pydantic Validation:** Every incoming payload is validated against strict Pydantic models before entering service business logic.
4. **Rate Limiting:** In production, reverse proxies enforce 60 requests/minute per IP on extraction endpoints.
