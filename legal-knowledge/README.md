> **⚠️ Disclaimer:** This is NOT legal advice. All templates and references are for informational purposes only. DealShield AI does not provide legal services. Users should consult qualified legal professionals for binding agreements.

## Planned Legal Sources

The following Indian legal frameworks are relevant to informal business agreements:

1. **Indian Contract Act, 1872**
   - Section 10: What agreements are contracts (free consent, lawful consideration, etc.)
   - Section 2(e): Agreement = promise + consideration
   - Section 2(h): Contract = agreement enforceable by law
   - Section 7: Acceptance must be absolute and unqualified
   - Section 23: Lawful consideration and object

2. **Information Technology Act, 2000**
   - Section 4: Legal recognition of electronic records
   - Section 5: Legal recognition of electronic signatures
   - Section 10A: Validity of contracts formed through electronic means
   - Section 65B: Admissibility of electronic records (Indian Evidence Act amendment)

3. **Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (now Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023)**
   - Section 65B (now Section 63): Electronic evidence admissibility requirements
   - Requirements for certification of electronic records

4. **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**
   - Relevant provisions for service agreements
   - Unfair contract terms
